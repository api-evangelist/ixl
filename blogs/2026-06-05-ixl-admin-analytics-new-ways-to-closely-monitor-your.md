Administrators now have enhanced access to deeper insights into student skill development through new filtering capabilities on Skill Usage and Skill Proficiency reports. Three additional filters reveal whether students are engaging with state standards-aligned content, grade-level appropriate skills, or personalized diagnostic recommendations. These tools allow educators to verify that their IXL implementation aligns with institutional objectives and track progress more precisely.
